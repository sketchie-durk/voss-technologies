VT-01 / R&D
Technical inquiry · Applied investigation

Research & Development

We investigate the behaviour of systems before treating assumptions as facts. Research work connects literature and protocol review with reproducible experiments, architecture analysis and implementation-level validation.

Research lifecycle
Research capabilities
01

Architecture & systems research

Comparative design studies, dependency mapping, component boundaries, failure analysis and technical decision records.

02

Protocol and implementation analysis

Trace execution paths, review interface contracts, inspect state transitions and identify mismatches between specification and implementation.

03

Applied experimentation

Define hypotheses, select measurable outcomes, build test harnesses, compare baselines and record reproducible findings.

04

Feasibility and proof-of-concept engineering

Reduce uncertain technical questions to bounded prototypes with explicit limitations and a path to production evaluation.

Research outputs
ArtifactPurposeExpected contents
Architecture noteMake system structure and trade-offs reviewable.Context, components, interfaces, alternatives, constraints.
Experiment recordMake results repeatable.Hypothesis, environment, method, observations, limitations.
Threat modelMake security assumptions explicit.Assets, actors, trust zones, attack paths, mitigations.
Technical assessmentSupport an engineering decision.Evidence, uncertainty, options, risks, recommended next test.
Research integrity rule: we distinguish measured results, reasoned hypotheses and unresolved questions. We do not present a conceptual diagram as a deployed architecture or an experiment as a universal guarantee.

VT-03 / SEC
Threat modelling · Exposure control · Trust boundaries

Security & Privacy Engineering

Security is treated as a system property: a consequence of boundaries, authorization, state validation, operational evidence and the behaviour of components when assumptions fail.

Trust-boundary model
Security services
TM

Threat modelling & architecture review

Assets, trust zones, attacker capabilities, abuse cases, attack paths and mitigation prioritization.

EXP

Exposure and privacy controls

Reduce unnecessary disclosure, map data flows, constrain access and define retention and handling boundaries.

NET

Network and endpoint security

Secure connectivity, traffic-path analysis, endpoint enforcement design and visibility into connection state.

OBS

Security observability

Correlated events, explicit protection states, alert conditions and evidence that distinguishes observed facts from inferred state.

Control objectives
ObjectiveImplementation patternsFailure posture
Identity & authorizationStrong authentication, scoped capabilities, server-side authorization.Deny when identity or scope cannot be established.
Input integritySchema validation, canonicalization, size and type constraints.Reject malformed or ambiguous input.
Freshness & replay resistanceNonces, TTLs, sequence numbers, idempotency keys.Reject expired, duplicate or out-of-order commands.
Operational truthFreshness metadata, source attribution, health checks and event correlation.Report unknown/stale state rather than false assurance.

Engagements are scoped to authorized environments and agreed test boundaries. A control design is not a certification or a guarantee of immunity.

Protocol integrity checklist
InvariantQuestion to resolve
Authorization bindingIs the signer or executor authorized for this exact action and parameter set?
Replay resistanceCan a valid command or proof be reused across transactions, chains, contracts or sessions?
Digest completenessDoes the commitment cover every parameter that changes execution meaning?
Verification boundaryIs verification performed by the intended trusted component, and does execution depend on its result?
Finality semanticsDoes the application distinguish submitted, included, confirmed and finalized states?
No blanket claim of audit, certification or production safety is implied. Chain-specific assumptions, bridge risks, contract scope and verification properties must be assessed per engagement.
